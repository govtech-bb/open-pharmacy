How this works

Government polyclinic pharmacies are free. Private pharmacies charge a small dispensing fee. Filter by parish or type to find one near you.

Where this information comes from

Government and Drug Service pharmacies come from the Drug Service register, verified May 2026. Pharmacies marked “Private (no benefit)” are drawn from a wider public pharmacy list and have not been confirmed with the Drug Service — they may still offer subsidised medication, so call to check.

Opening hours and Drug Service participation can change — call ahead to confirm.

Prescription slip colours

The colour of your prescription slip — the paper your doctor or clinic gives you — can affect which pharmacy can fill it.
